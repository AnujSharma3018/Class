EYE HEALTH TIPS

Take Care Of Your Eyes

Simple habits that can support healthy vision.

💻

20-20-20 Rule

During prolonged screen use, every 20 minutes look at something about 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds.

🥗

Healthy Nutrition

Include a balanced diet with vegetables, fruits and nutrients that support eye health.

🩺

Regular Eye Checkups

Get regular eye examinations as recommended by your eye care professional.
