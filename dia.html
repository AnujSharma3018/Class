UNDERSTANDING DIABETES

Different Types of Diabetes

Learn about common types of diabetes.

01

Type 1 Diabetes

An autoimmune condition in which the body produces little or no insulin.

02

Type 2 Diabetes

A condition where the body does not use insulin effectively or produce enough of it.

03

Gestational Diabetes

High blood glucose that develops during pregnancy and requires medical care.
