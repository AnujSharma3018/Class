Common Heart Attack Warning Signs

⚠️ Chest pain, pressure or discomfort

⚠️ Pain in one or both arms, back, neck or jaw

⚠️ Shortness of breath

⚠️ Cold sweat, nausea or dizziness

⚠️ Unusual weakness or fatigue

🚨 Medical Emergency

These symptoms may indicate a heart attack. Call your local emergency service immediately. Do not wait for an AI response.
